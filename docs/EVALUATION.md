## Design decisions this evaluation drove

* **Drums.** A first band-wise spectral-flux detector over-triggered badly: kick F1 0.63 and snare F1 0.49, from cross-band leakage. It was replaced by semi-adaptive NMF (F1 1.0 on every class here).
* **Bass.** The default moved from pYIN to band-limited Basic Pitch (F1 0.91 vs 0.79). pYIN merges repeated same-pitch eighth notes. It is still one click away for legato lines.
* **Silent stems.** Separation randomness (`shifts=1`) made the near-empty vocal stem vary between −43 and −65 dBFS. Deterministic separation plus a mix-relative silence rule stops "notes" being transcribed out of bleed.
* **Tempo.** Refining tempo from the median beat interval was biased by hop quantisation (117.5 instead of 120 BPM on a click track). It now uses a least-squares fit of beat time against beat index (96.01 vs 96.0 BPM here).
